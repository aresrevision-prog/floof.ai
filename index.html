🔒 Privacy notice

  

  
Floof AI by MagicWinter Studio · last updated 11 November 2026

  
The short version

  
  • Most of Floof runs inside your browser. Your chats, notes, memory and settings stay on your device.
    • 
  
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, nothing sold.
    • 
  
  • Your camera and screen are only used when you turn them on, and only the frame you choose to solve is ever used.

  
Stored on your device (this browser only)

  
  • Chats, knowledge/notes, the grid, things Floof learns about you (Me tab), settings and any API keys you paste.
    • 
  
  • Downloaded tools: on-device brains, the dictionary, the text reader and Python.
    • 
  
  • Delete any time: delete chats, use Forget everything in the Me tab, Reset downloads, or clear this site's data in your browser.

  
Accounts

  
  • MagicWinter account: your username, a one-way scrambled (bcrypt) copy of your password and sign-in tokens are kept on the studio's account server (Supabase). Your real password is never stored. Delete it in the Me tab.
    • 
  
  • Ares account (Edu mode): your Ares username and password are checked by Ares Revision's own server. Floof doesn't store your Ares password.
    • 
  
  • Device-only account: stays on this device, nothing is sent anywhere.

  
Camera 📷 and screen 🖥

  
  • Floof asks your browser first; you can stop at any time with ⏹. The live video is never recorded or uploaded.
    • 
  
  • When you press 📸 Solve, only that one picture is used: it's read on your device, or — if you've chosen Gemini — sent to Google to be understood. A small thumbnail is kept in your chat.

  
Services Floof only uses when you choose them

  
  • Floof Cloud (MagicWinter accounts) and Athena (Ares accounts): your messages, and photos you choose to solve, go through the studio's / Ares' server to Google Gemini to get an answer. These use Google's free tier, where Google may use what's sent to improve its AI — so don't share private or personal details.
    • 
  
  • Google Gemini / OpenRouter (☁ brain): your messages, and photos you choose to solve, are sent to that company to get an answer, under their privacy policies. Google requires the key holder to be 18+.
    • 
  
  • Your own PC (Ollama): stays on your network.
    • 
  
  • Wikipedia (🌐 web and learning), dictionaryapi.dev (word meanings).
    • 
  
  • Pollinations (free backup brain, used only when no other brain is working): your messages are sent to it to get an answer.
    • 
  
  • Downloads from jsDelivr and Hugging Face (fonts, brains, dictionary, text reader, Python). These see an ordinary download request, like any website.

  
Children

  
  • Ares accounts use 📚 Edu mode: study-focused and age-appropriate answers.
    • 
  
  • Floof refuses to remember passwords, phone numbers, emails or addresses. Don't share private details with any AI.
